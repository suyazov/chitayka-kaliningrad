# IMAGE_PROMPTS

## Сгенерированный декоративный фон

- Инструмент: встроенный `image_gen`.
- Результат: `generated-brand-background.png`.
- Роль: фоновый слой для обложки кейса; интерфейс сайта и весь текст на финальной инфографике наложены отдельно из реальных скриншотов.

### Финальный prompt

```text
Use case: infographic-diagram.
Asset type: decorative background layer for a portfolio case cover about a children's education center website.
Create a polished airy premium landscape background in the supplied mascot's friendly brand style. Preserve one small recognizable mascot at the lower-right edge reading the green book: white oval body, purple antennae and outlines, yellow-purple wings, large purple eyes. Use an ivory-to-pale-lilac gradient with soft layered hills, open books, stars, paper airplane, mint notebook and rounded translucent shapes near the outer edges. Leave the central and left 65% calm and uncluttered for later typography and real website screenshots. Refined soft 3D clay/paper illustration, subtle depth and soft shadows. Palette: deep purple, lilac, mint, warm yellow, cream, restrained coral. No text, no logo, no words, no people, no UI mockups, no watermark, no busy confetti, no dark background, no duplicated limbs.
```
